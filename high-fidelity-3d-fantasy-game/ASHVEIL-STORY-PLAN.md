# Ashveil — The Sundered Flame (current story)

## Premise

Ten years before Minslaire's journey, Kinter of Elderville — Minslaire's father — is dispatched by the Elders on a mission most assume is a quiet death sentence: find life beyond Gretriyon. A few months into the journey, he slips down a rock slide into a river, is carried over a waterfall, and wakes on the shore of Uoka — a realm sealed behind walls of ash and failing Life Armour.

Uoka's king, **Aldrich**, is a good ruler in a dying house. His people wear heavy **Life Armour**, bulkier than Gretriyon lifesuits; without it, nobody breathes the poisoned air. Their ancient **Raeku** — the elemental box powering every suit in the realm — is being drained by **Malenkar**, a warlord who fled into the Ashveil with the half-shattered core.

## The Long Fall (prologue — playable, ~11 minutes)

Authored cinematic with short moments of control: forest → rock slide → waterfall → Uoka's shore. Guards surround him. Their commander **Lyekai** asks: "Who are you, and why are you here?" Kinter: "My name is Kinter, from Elderville. The Elders sent me to find life beyond Gretriyon. I think I just found it." He lowers his second blade and lets them take him.

Escort across the drawbridge. Throne hall. Sentry forces him to one knee before Aldrich.

**Aldrich:** "You're a fighter, aren't you?" / "Yes." / "You're from Gretriyon, right?" / "H-how did you know?" / Life Armour, the wars that split the world, the Raeku. "If you venture into the Ashveil and reclaim the Shards of Light, we can channel their power — strengthen your gear, reinforce my own armor, and break Malenkar's hold on the Raeku. Do that, and I will give you everything you need to find your way home."

## The King's Trial

Aldrich demands proof before staking his kingdom on a stranger: the Hall of Blades effigies, then the Terrace of Trials. Completing the Trial opens the Ashveil Gauntlet.

## The Ashveil Gauntlet

The Ashveil opens fully. Eight **Shards of Light** are scattered among eight guardians, one per region — hunt them in **any order**, or march on Malenkar's citadel immediately. Both are legal.

| Region | Guardian | Lair — the dungeon |
|---|---|---|
| The Iron Dunes | The Rusted Colossus | **The Tank Cemetery** — a pre-war graveyard of colossal half-buried hulls |
| The Sunken Bastion | The Hollow Captain | **The Drowned Keep** — four towers around a black moat, one face collapsed |
| The Cinderwood | The Ash-Stalker | **The Ashen Chapel** — a roofless nave of broken columns and a burning rose window |
| The Glass Crags | The Prism Warden | **The Obsidian Ziggurat** — a stepped sun pyramid of fused volcanic glass |
| The Slag Marshes | The Molten Leviathan | **The Drowned Nave** — a sunken temple of drowned colonnades |
| The Whispering Spire | The Gale Sentinel | **The Turbine Cathedral** — a cathedral rebuilt around the last wind turbine |
| The Forgotten Foundry | The Iron Forgemaster | **The Foundry Cathedral** — furnace stacks, a great kiln and a dead conveyor |
| The Ashen Cathedral | The Pale Herald | **The Pale Nave** — the ruined gateway on the road to Malenkar |

Each lair stands on levelled ground (see `FLATTENS` in `terrain.ts`) so the architecture rises true.

Each Shard strengthens Kinter's gear **and** Aldrich's armor — and the Raeku's charge back home.

## The finale — the king charges in

Malenkar waits in the Sunken Cathedral. When his blood is first drawn, **Aldrich charges into the fight himself, one final time**. If all eight Shards are gathered, he brings the full weight of the Raeku to bear. If not, he burns bright but falls quickly.

Aldrich dies either way. His last words pass the crown: **"You are... the king of Uoka."**

Malenkar falls after.

## Epilogue

Kinter of Elderville — the father who never came home — becomes King of Uoka. He cannot leave: the Raeku would go unguarded, and the darkness Malenkar served would spill across the Ashveil and consume Gretriyon too. The First Blade stays with Minslaire. Ten years later, when Elderville sends another son into the dark, Minslaire will never know why his father never came home.

## Design notes

- **Rush option:** Malenkar can be challenged at any point after the Trial. Survivable with skill, nearly hopeless without the Shards.
- **Eight guardians** share one boss AI with distinct stats, tints, lairs, and Shard rewards.
- **The Raeku** is technology, not magic: suits, boxes, custodial systems. No literal incantations.
- **Ashbearer** is the title the court of Uoka gives Kinter.
